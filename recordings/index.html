Spatial UI cues

Cues that live on the room instead of on a panel: they take the shape of the thing they are about (its box, its footprint, its edge, a doorway, the floor). Each is drawn on a real object of a recording. Each says when to use it and what it is for; which one appears is decided from the target and its data, not fixed per MOD.
